# Phase 2 results — vision→text bridge (1st CI campaign)

Run on GitHub Actions `ubuntu-latest` (Python 3.11, CPU), 2026-08-30.
Head commit: `d32f951`. Extractors pulled from the Hugging Face hub at the time
of the run:

| Role | Model | Revision |
|---|---|---|
| Vision (source) | `HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM2-500M-Video-Instruct` | `7b375e1b` (SigLIP, 768-dim, patch states mean-pooled to one row/image) |
| Text (target) | `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M-Instruct` | `main` (mean-pooled caption embeddings, 960-dim) |

## Protocol

- Calibration set: 24 synthetic image+caption pairs (`bench/calib`, generated by
  `scripts/make_synthetic_calib.py`, seed 7: deterministic shapes/colors/patterns
  with matching captions).
- `CalibrationBridge.fit(src, tgt, lam=1e-3)`: whitening (train stats) →
  Procrustes-init `(R, scale)` → ridge `A`.
- Deterministic 19/5 train/test split (`split_by_key`, seed 42), evaluated on the
  5 held-out pairs vs **zero** and **random-init** baselines.
- Gates (per stage-1a of `PROPOSAL.md`): `cosine_ridge - cosine_zero ≥ +0.15`,
  `cosine_ridge - cosine_random ≥ +0.10`, `rel_fro_ridge ≤ 0.60`.

## Interpretation

- **Pipeline is end-to-end healthy**: content-addressed cache populated from the
  real HF extractors (~1 min on CI CPU), bridge fitted, held-out eval ran, and
  results were surfaced + artifacted. Lint/typecheck/unit are green on 3.11/3.12.
- **The synthetic calibration does not show directional alignment.**
  `cosine_ridge` (+0.02) barely edges out the zero baseline and sits below the
  random-init floor; the unconstrained ridge is no better than an isotropic
  residual. This is expected and useful: generated captions do not carry enough
  shared signal to move SigLIP mean states and SmolLM2 mean-pooled caption
  embeddings into the same direction — synthetic data only validates the
  machinery, not the physics of the alignment.
